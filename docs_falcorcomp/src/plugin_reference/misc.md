# Miscellaneous

Render passes that help to look at a scene rather than simulate a sensor:

- [`LaserPositionViewer`](misc/LaserPositionViewer.md): draws where the laser is, its beam and the spot it lights,
  over an image.
- [`InlinePathTracer`](misc/InlinePathTracer.md): a plain path tracer lit by the laser, without time of flight.
